# Realms & Res Publica

A NeoForge 1.21.1 mod (Java 21) that turns Minecraft villagers into a living society.

- **Seven roles**: farmer, forester, miner, guard, steward, builder, diplomat — each with its own real in-world behavior.
- **Three gauges per villager**: morale, satiety, loyalty (visible with `/respublica inspect`, saved in the villager's data).
- **Organic demography**: births depend on the food really stored in the village barrel and on the real number of beds; hunger and overcrowding make villagers leave.
- **Crises with real causes**: famine, overpopulation, insecurity → contestation → exile (a dissident camp is built) → civil war → truce (reconciliation or autonomy).
- **Town Hall and community store**: a lectern on a gold block marks the village core (`/rrp townhall set|info|build`). Foresters, miners and farmers deposit wood, stone, ores and crops into chests/barrels next to it; the steward draws from the same store, and a new chest is placed when it is full (`max_chests`).
- **Autonomous expansion**: once the stock (`expansion_wood_quota`, `expansion_stone_quota`) and population (`expansion_population_quota`) quotas are met, the steward orders a building (house, big house, farm, workshop, tower) from a biome-adaptive pool of generated `.nbt` blueprints. Builders level the terrain and place the blocks row by row, fully on their own; construction resumes after a restart.
- **Roads**: dirt paths between the Town Hall, buildings and neighbouring villages/camps (`road_link_distance`), paved with cobblestone/stone bricks when stone and population allow (`road_pave_stone_stock`, `road_pave_population`).
- **Defenses**: watchtowers, an oak palisade then stone walls with gates at road crossings, started by population or threats (`defense_population`, `defense_threats`, `defense_ring_radius`, `defense_stone_stock`, `defense_stone_population`); guards man the towers.
- **Territory and borders**: each Town Hall, outpost, camp and watchtower projects influence over chunks (`territory_hall_radius`, `territory_outpost_radius`); a faction owns a chunk above `territory_owner_threshold`, and a chunk is contested within `territory_contest_margin`. Border posts mark the limits and builders place them. `/rrp territory` shows the map, doctrines and relations.
- **Provocation and border war**: villagers working in a neighbour's chunk raise provocation (`provocation_per_act`); the owner warns (`provocation_warn`), guards expel (`provocation_expel`) and a border war breaks out (`provocation_war`). It decays by `provocation_decay_s`; `territory_trespass` lets villagers work abroad freely.
- **Doctrines**: fixed at founding from the surrounding biome. Merchant city (diplomats, markets, never trespasses), warlike empire (guards, miners, barracks, large claims), isolationist theocracy (monuments, expels strangers, walls first).
- **Inter-faction relations**: trade, neutral or hostile stance from the doctrine pair, degraded by border incidents (3+ = hostile); diplomats gain relations faster when trading.
- **Relation index (-100..+100)**: every pair of villages has a numeric relation, seeded from the doctrine pair (`relation_base_trade`, `relation_base_hostile`), lowered by border incidents (`relation_incident_penalty`) and drifting back to neutral (`relation_decay_s`). At or above `relation_trade_at` the stance is TRADE, at or below `relation_hostile_at` it is HOSTILE.
- **Caravans**: a village with a surplus (`caravan_surplus`) sends a trader with `caravan_escorts` guards to a friendly partner in deficit (`caravan_deficit`), at most `caravan_max_load` items, every `caravan_interval_s`. Delivery raises the relation (`caravan_relation_gain`); an ambush (`caravan_ambush_chance`) or timeout (`caravan_timeout_s`) lowers it (`caravan_relation_loss`).
- **Treaties and war declarations**: states TRADE_TREATY, DEFENSIVE_ALLIANCE (relation >= `diplomacy_alliance_at` and a common enemy) and WAR. After `diplomacy_war_incidents` incidents an ultimatum is issued; war follows after `diplomacy_war_delay_s` unless a calming gesture (`diplomacy_calm_gain`) or a diplomat prevents it. A village in critical food (`diplomacy_critical_food`) avoids war.
- **Mobilisation**: at war, up to `mobilization_share` of the population forms a regiment, equipped from the warehouse with locally forged gear (`mobilization_sword_ore`, `mobilization_armor_ore`, `mobilization_bow_wood`).
- **Sieges**: attacking builders assemble a battering ram (breaks doors and gates) then a catapult (breaches wall blocks) near the enemy border; defending guards destroy engines. Each siege may break at most `siege_block_cap` blocks and never digs below the village floor (`siege_*` keys).
- **War outcomes**: after `war_resolve_min_s` (at most `war_resolve_max_s`) the power ratio decides: REPELLED, PILLAGE (`war_pillage_at`, loot `war_loot_share` capped by `war_loot_cap`), ANNEXATION (`war_annex_at`: claims transferred, periodic tribute `war_tribute_share` capped by `war_tribute_cap` every `war_tribute_period_s`) or DESTRUCTION (`war_destroy_at`, needs a full breach cap: the hall is removed and the population merges into the nearest village). A ceasefire restores peace (`war_peace_gain`).
- **Four player roles** (`/rrp role set <observer|sovereign|mercenary|merchant>`, switch cooldown `role_switch_cooldown_s`; observers are never targeted; `/rrp role purse deposit|withdraw <n>` moves emeralds to a persistent purse):
  - **Sovereign**: found a village by completing a Town Hall (lectern on gold, `found_*` keys); `/rrp law` (borders, building ban, conscription %, embargo on a village), `/rrp tax set <food|wood|ore|stone> <%>` (`tax_*` keys), `/rrp plan` urban zones (residential, farm, market, barracks, forbidden) drawn with a wooden shovel, respected by the expansion planner; `/rrp register` diplomatic register with clickable proposals (trade, alliance, ceasefire, ultimatum).
  - **Mercenary**: `/rrp contract list|status|accept <n>|abandon <n>`; villages post camp-clearing and war contracts with the pay escrowed in emeralds (`contract_*` keys); reputation and relation shifts on completion.
  - **Merchant**: `/rrp market prices|buy|sell <food|wood|ore|stone> <n>`: prices follow each village's stock versus need, with a spread and a daily saturation cap (`market_*` keys); real goods move to and from the village warehouse.
  - **Political map**: right-click a cartography table to get a filled map of factions, borders, war zones and caravan routes.
- **Level of detail (scales to hundreds of villages)**: a village is ACTIVE while its Town Hall chunk is entity-ticking (a player is near or the chunk is force-loaded) and ABSTRACT otherwise. An abstract village keeps living in memory: seeded probabilistic harvests per role and biome yield (`abstract_*`), eating, morale, births, armies and caravans that march as points on the map (`abstract_march_speed`, `abstract_road_factor`) and materialise when they reach a live area. Becoming active again is immediate (the stock is reconciled with the real warehouse); turning abstract waits `lod_grace_s`. Keys: `lod_enabled` (default on), `lod_check_ticks`, `lod_grace_s` (the delay before turning abstract; raise it to avoid flip-flopping at a chunk-loading boundary). Biome override: `biome_overrides` (`biome_id=KIND` list) for any modded biome the classifier gets wrong. Commands: `/rrp lod` (list), `/rrp lod force <village> active|abstract|auto`, `/rrp lod perf [reset]` (mod cost per tick).
- **Biome compatibility (Terralith and other biome mods)**: doctrine, yields and building style come from a classifier that tries, in order, the `biome_overrides` config list, the biome tags (`is_forest`, `is_mountain`, `is_ocean`...), keywords in the biome name, then temperature/rainfall. An unknown biome never breaks anything (it just gives no doctrine hint). To force a modded biome: `biome_overrides: "terralith:volcanic_crater=mountain, mymod:glow_marsh=swamp"` (kinds: plains, forest, taiga, jungle, swamp, mountain, badlands, desert, savanna, ocean, river, beach).
- **Physical logistics (no teleported resources)**: a villager takes or stores items only within `chest_reach` (2 blocks) of the chest/barrel. Sequence: walk to the container, the lid opens with the vanilla open/close sounds, the villager rummages for `rummage_min_ticks`..`rummage_max_ticks` (crouched, looking at the chest), then the items appear in its inventory and main hand (vanilla renders the hand slot, no client asset needed). A worker carries at most `carry_capacity` units (32 = half a stack); carried items survive a restart.
  - **Farmer, forester, miner**: harvest, logs and ore go into the carried load; the worker walks to the warehouse to deposit when full or when no more work is in range. The stock never changes while the worker is far from the container.
  - **Builder**: nothing is withdrawn at site start. Each construction step consumes carried units; when short, the builder stops, walks back to the warehouse, loads and returns (huts, roads, palisade and walls follow the same rule).
  - **Empty chest = active wait**: the worker complains once to the steward (chat line and a shortage flag the steward reads), clears grass and bushes nearby, then sits; a seated worker only looks around again (plants) every `wait_retry_s`; as soon as the chest holds what it needs it resumes.
- **Armoury and local smith**: a designated barrel next to the Town Hall (saved with the village). The miner is the smith: it carries ore from the warehouse and forges iron armour, sharpened swords and bows (`forge_ticks` per piece) into the armoury. Whenever its kit is incomplete (and no monster is near) a guard walks to the armoury, equips itself and takes `guard_rations` food rations; a guard never patrols unarmed (it waits at the armoury and the village is warned when it is empty; the smith forges weapons first, then armour, and a guard attacked by a monster still defends itself with its fists).
- **Martial guards**: guards never enter the vanilla panic state and never flee zombies, skeletons or spiders. Sentinels hold towers and gates; patrols walk in pairs along the border. Archers shoot real arrows up to `guard_ranged_range` (24) blocks and reload crouched behind the battlements (`guard_reload_ticks`). Melee guards raise the shield (projectiles from the front arc `guard_shield_arc` are cancelled) and advance together.
- **Anti-creeper**: archers shoot a creeper before it ignites (it is the top priority within `guard_creeper_alert` blocks); closer than `guard_creeper_close` a melee guard holds its shield and does not step back: explosion damage is cut by `guard_creeper_reduction` % and knockback is cancelled.
- **Encirclement**: against `guard_encircle_min` hostiles or more, two guards pin the nearest enemy behind their shields while a third walks around to a flank point `guard_flank_distance` blocks behind it and strikes from behind.
- **Tactical retreat**: at or under `guard_retreat_pct` % health (25) a guard withdraws behind the line of its comrades, goes to the barracks/armoury and heals with the `guard_heal_items` (the "bandages": nothing produces them, so stock them in the barracks/armoury barrel yourself), restoring `guard_heal_amount` each, or slowly without. No disorderly flight.
- **Civilian state machine**: ALERT (bell rung once per village) -> SHELTER (bed, house or Town Hall) -> WAIT_SECURED -> back to work, released only after `civilian_release_ticks` without any hostile nearby, so after the guards cleared the threat. Children are included.
- All balancing lives in `config.yml`; messages are in French (`lang_fr.yml`).

## Install
- **Solo / LAN (integrated server)**: install NeoForge 21.1.x for Minecraft 1.21.1, copy `deliverable/RealmsResPublica.jar` into the `mods/` folder, start the game and open a world. Commands (`/rrp`) need cheats enabled.
- **Dedicated server**: run the NeoForge 21.1.x server installer, copy the jar into the server's `mods/`, start once, accept the EULA. Give yourself op.
- The first launch copies the annotated config to `config/respublica/config.yml` (a copy is in `deliverable/config.yml`); edit it and run `/rrp reload`.

## Build from source
`./gradlew build` (JDK 21) -> `build/libs/RealmsResPublica.jar`. Simulator: `bash tools/run_sim.sh`. Headless GameTests: `./gradlew runGameTestServer`.
