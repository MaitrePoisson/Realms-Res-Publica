# Decisions
- 2026-10-23: platform = Paper 1.20.4 plugin (Java 17 available; no modified client; testable headless).
- No randomness in the core (reproducibility); disobedience is deterministic via a morale threshold.
- Civil war: villagers have no native attack; guards advance toward the nearest enemy and deal damage with a cooldown + particles.
- No screenshots (headless server): SVG reports from the simulator and selftest logs stand in for them.
- Communal warehouse = barrel designated by command.
- M6: each crisis level has a "gauge" (timer full on entry) that empties at `crisis_recovery_factor` when the cause disappears; empty ⇒ drop one level (slow recovery, no randomness).
- M6: exile = average loyalty < `exile_loyalty` in CONTESTATION for `exile_after`; ≥ 2 villagers required, at least 1 remains; the camp (`<id>-camp`, `parentId`) is created in the core with a food share, beds = number of exiles, `camp_guard_share` of exiles as guards.
- M6: fighters = GUARD role; no war if a side has none; war ends by elimination when a side has no more. Truce = living diplomat (on either side) and village `relations` ≥ `truce_relations`; the camp stays autonomous (reconciliation = M16 on the Bukkit side). A village crosses only one level per step.
- M7: defaults rebalanced so that famine and overpopulation lead to exile (otherwise loyalty convergence cancelled the loss): loyalty_low_morale 40→55, loyalty_low_morale_decay_per_s 0.1→0.4. Virtual economy: 0.8 food/farmer/min, builders only if beds < population+2.
- M8: selftest launched from the console (`respublica selftest` sent by tools/run_server_test.sh after "Done"), result in selftest-result.json read by the script; `/respublica tick n` advances TickService (LongConsumer listeners).
- M9: a village is created only by discovery (≥2 free villagers in the radius + ≥1 bed); beds are counted via tile entities of loaded chunks (budget max_scan_blocks_per_tick).

- M10: food eaten by the gauges (Gauges.feed) is removed from the real barrel by GaugeTicker; the Steward (M12) will add the physical gesture without doubling consumption.
- M10: a villager outside loaded chunks leaves the village state and is reread from its PDC on return (written on unload and every persistence_flush_s).
- M10: babies are not adopted; they will be at maturity (M14).
- Role behaviors (M11) use forced runOnce for tests; cadence goes through BehaviorScheduler.onTicks (resetClock in test).
- M12: the steward completes the automatic meal of Gauges.feed (threshold + 20) with real food taken from the barrel; the guard strikes for 20 damage.
- M15: exiles are teleported in front of their tent (HOME/JOB_SITE/POTENTIAL_JOB_SITE/MEETING_POINT memories cleared) rather than directed by pathfinder: deterministic on a headless server, and vanilla AI cannot bring them back.
- M15: the camp (radius 20) is placed at max(camp_distance, village radius + 24) from the village, in the best of 8 directions (penalty: water/lava/tree/ice under the 91 sampled columns, slope > 2); 12 tents at most, the remaining exiles share beds (camp overpopulation).
- M15: the food taken away is actually moved (removal from the village barrel, bread deposited in the camp barrel).
- M15b: visuals = HTML/SVG page generated by the plugin during selftest (no Minecraft client); PNG via Edge headless, optional (ignored if absent).
- M16: during war, the village's membership (VillageSite.reach) is widened up to the camp so that its guards can reach it without being "lost" by sync; reconciliation = the camp returns (camp barrel left in place), autonomy = parentId cleared.
- 2026-10-23: resume request handled by keeping the existing plan (M21, M22 remaining); work and commits stay in this folder's own git repo.
- 2026-10-23: NeoForge port: rrp.core/rrp.sim untouched; Paper code kept until M30 as reference, then deleted; Java 21 toolchain in $TOOLS/jdk21.
- Town Hall is a vanilla structure marker (lectern on gold block), not a registered block, to keep the mod server-side only.
- M42: provocation state is volatile (decays on its own, nothing persisted); trespass is a global config flag until M43 makes it a doctrine property; border war reuses WarService.fight without the camp clamp.
- M43: doctrine modifiers are enum constants, not config.yml keys (keeps SimConfig tests and config simple).
- M44: no separate road order for the merchant doctrine (MARKET only); empire "claims neighbour borders" through claimRadiusFactor 1.5 in the influence map.
- M52: tribute is a capped share of the loser's warehouse stock each war_tribute_period_s (no production meter exists); destruction merges the population into the nearest other village.
- M53: the war run is a separate script (tools/run_war_server.sh) called by check.sh after the main server phases; builders (batisseur) are required for the siege, guards for the regiment.
- M54: real-server phase 5 (biome doctrine) is flaky (failed 2 of 4 full runs, passes on rerun); accepted as a known P2, rerun before concluding.
- M62: player-role flows cannot be driven from a headless server console (no player); observed via GameTests with mock players, console-driven village/war flows via real server runs. Documented in TEST_GUIDE.
